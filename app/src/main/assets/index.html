Viscosity figures are typical values from each maker's product data sheet (ASTM D445, 100°C); numbers vary by market and formulation revision. The result estimates the warm (100°C) viscosity of the mixture — it does not predict the cold-start "W" rating or the wet-clutch friction (JASO MA/MA2) rating. Motul, Castrol and Shell all state their bike oils may be mixed, but blending full- and semi-synthetic also averages their additive packs; for best results use one full fill of a single grade.
